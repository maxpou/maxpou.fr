⚠️ The blind spots of this calculator

This tool calculates theoretical compounding and does not account for real-world factors like taxes (tax drag), which reduce your actual returns. It also assumes a smooth, fixed annual return, ignoring market volatility (the linearity bias) and the fact that inflation will erode your final purchasing power over time. For a more realistic estimate, consider lowering your expected return rate by a few percentage points to factor in these costs.
